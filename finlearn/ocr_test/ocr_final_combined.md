1.1.1 经济周期基础知识（Fundamentals of the Business Cycle）
Stages of the Business Cycle
The business cycle refers to the recurring, irregular fluctuations in aggregate economic activity around the economy’s long-term potential growth trend. It is officially determined by the U.S. National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER), which defines a "recession" as a significant decline in economic activity spread across the economy, lasting more than a few months—encompassing production, employment, and income. The business cycle’s duration varies (typically 2–10 years) and is driven by shifts in aggregate demand and aggregate supply.
商业周期是指总体经济活动围绕经济长期潜在增长趋势反复出现的、不规则的波动。它由美国国家经济研究局（NBER）正式确定，该局将“衰退”定义为广泛蔓延于整个经济的经济活动显著下降，持续数月以上——包括生产、就业和收入。商业周期的持续时间各不相同（通常为2至10年），由总需求和总供给的变化驱动。

1.1.3 经济周期的关键驱动因素（Key Drivers of the U.S. Business Cycle）
Factors directly influencing U.S. business cycle fluctuations
直接影响美国商业周期波动的因素
- Consumer Spending: Consumer spending accounts for approximately 68%–70% of U.S. GDP, making it the primary driver of aggregate demand. Changes in consumer confidence (tied to employment security and wage growth, per textbook) directly impact retail sales, housing demand, and discretionary spending (e.g., autos, electronics)—critical for U.S. equity sectors like consumer staples and discretionary.
- 消费者支出：消费者支出约占美国GDP的68% - 70%，是总需求的主要驱动力。消费者信心的变化（根据教科书，与就业保障和工资增长相关）直接影响零售销售、住房需求和可自由支配支出（例如，汽车、电子产品）——对美国消费必需品和可自由支配等股票部门至关重要。

企业投资：包括对机械、设备和商业地产的支出。美国商业投资对利率（美联储政策）、企业利润率和技术创新（例如，2023 - 2024年与AI相关的投资热潮）敏感。

Monetary Policy (Federal Reserve): Fed’s adjustments to the federal funds rate and balance sheet (quantitative easing/QE, quantitative tightening/QT) directly influence borrowing costs for businesses and consumers, asset prices (stocks, bonds), and inflation.

货币政策（美联储）：美联储对联邦基金利率和资产负债表（量化宽松/QE、量化紧缩/QT）的调整直接影响企业和消费者的借贷成本、资产价格（股票、债券）以及通货膨胀。

Net Exports: Tied to the U.S. dollar exchange rate and global demand. A stronger USD (e.g., 2022) reduces U.S. export competitiveness, while weak global demand (e.g., 2023 Eurozone slowdown) weighs on U.S. export growth.

净出口：与美元汇率和全球需求相关。美元走强（如2022年）会削弱美国出口竞争力，而全球需求疲软（如2023年欧元区经济放缓）则会拖累美国出口增长。

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) | 国内生产总值 (GDP)
[Coincident Indicator]
[同步指标]
- Definition: The total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country’s borders in a given period. The BEA releases quarterly data with three revisions; real GDP (adjusted for inflation) is the key metric for cycle analysis (nominal GDP is less useful for trend identification).

定义：在给定时期内，一个国家境内生产的所有最终商品和服务的总市场价值。美国经济分析局（BEA）每季度发布数据，并有三次修订；实际GDP（经通胀调整）是周期分析的关键指标（名义GDP在趋势识别方面的作用较小）。

- Explanation: The broadest measure of economic activity. Real GDP growth above 2% (U.S. potential growth rate) indicates expansion; growth below 1% signals slowing activity; negative growth for ≥2 quarters is a common recession proxy (supplementary to NBER’s definition). Market reactions: strong GDP may raise Fed hike expectations (pressuring bonds); weak growth may signal easing (bonds outperform).

解释：经济活动最广泛的衡量指标。实际GDP增长高于2%（美国潜在增长率）表明经济扩张；增长低于1%则预示经济活动放缓；连续两个或更多季度的负增长是常见的衰退指标（作为美国国家经济研究局定义的补充）。市场反应：强劲的GDP可能提高美联储加息预期（给债券带来压力）；经济疲软可能预示政策宽松（债券表现优于其他资产）。

Unemployment Rate | 失业率
[Lagging Indicator]
[滞后指标]
- Definition: BLS monthly data measuring the percentage of the labor force (employed + actively seeking work) that is unemployed; excludes discouraged workers (a key limitation noted in the textbook).

定义：BLS月度数据，用于衡量劳动力（就业者+积极求职人员）中失业人员的百分比；不包括灰心丧气的劳动者（这是教科书中提到的一个关键局限性）。

- Explanation: The U.S. natural rate of unemployment (full employment) is 4%–4.5%. Unemployment below this range creates wage inflation pressures; above 6% (e.g., 14.7% in 2020) signals recessionary conditions.

解释：美国自然失业率（充分就业）为4% - 4.5%，低于这一范围的失业率会产生工资通胀压力；高于6%（例如2020年为14.7%）表明经济处于衰退状态。

# Consumer Price Index (CPI) | 消费者价格指数 (CPI)
## [Lagging Indicator]
### [滞后指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BLS data measuring the change in prices of a fixed basket of goods/services (food, energy, housing, healthcare) purchased by urban consumers. Core CPI (excludes food/energy) is preferred for trend analysis due to volatility (textbook clarification).
  - **定义**：每月BLS数据，用于衡量城市消费者购买的一篮子固定商品/服务（食品、能源、住房、医疗保健）价格的变化。由于波动性，核心CPI（不包括食品/能源）更适合用于趋势分析（教科书说明）。

- **Explanation**: The primary market-watched inflation gauge. Core CPI growth above 3% triggers Fed hawkishness, below 1% raises deflation risks. Critical for bonds (inflation erodes fixed returns) and stocks (high inflation compresses margins).
  - **解释**：主要受市场关注的通胀指标。核心CPI涨幅超过3%会引发美联储的鹰派立场；低于1%会增加通缩风险。这对债券（通胀会侵蚀固定回报）和股票（高通胀会压缩利润率）至关重要。

# Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) Price Index | 个人消费支出 (PCE) 价格指数
## [Lagging Indicator]
### [滞后指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BEA data measuring prices of goods/services purchased by consumers, with a more flexible basket than CPI (e.g., includes employer-paid healthcare). Core PCE (excludes food/energy) is the Fed’s preferred inflation gauge.
  - **定义**：美国经济分析局（BEA）的月度数据，用于衡量消费者购买商品和服务的价格，其商品篮子比CPI更灵活（例如，包括雇主支付的医疗保险费用）。核心个人消费支出（PCE）（不包括食品/能源）是美联储首选的通胀指标。

- **Explanation**: Directly guides Fed policy (2% target). Core PCE above 2% sustains hawkishness; below 2% supports dovishness. PCE is less volatile than CPI, leading to more muted but longer-lasting market reactions.
  - **解释**：直接指导美联储政策（2%目标）。核心PCE高于2%会维持鹰派立场；低于2%的支持鸽派立场。PCE的波动性低于CPI，导致市场反应更为温和但持续时间更长。

# ISM Manufacturing PMI | ISM制造业PMI
## [Leading Indicator]
### [领先指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly ISM survey of manufacturing firms, measuring new orders, production, employment, supplier deliveries, and inventories. A reading >50 indicates expansion; <50 indicates contraction.
  - **定义**：每月对制造业企业进行的ISM调查，衡量新订单、生产、就业、供应商交货和库存情况。读数>50表示扩张；<50表示收缩。

- **Explanation**: A leading indicator (PMI >55 signals strong industrial growth; <45 suggests manufacturing recession). The new orders sub-index is a key predictor of future GDP growth.
  - **解释**：领先指标（PMI>55表明工业强劲增长；<45则暗示制造业衰退）。新订单分项指数是未来GDP增长的关键预测指标。

---

# Retail Sales | 零售销售
## [Coincident Indicator]
### [同步指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BLS data measuring total sales of retail establishments (including food services and autos). Core retail sales (excludes autos/gasoline) is a better proxy for consumer spending trends.
  - **定义**：每月BLS数据，衡量零售企业（包括餐饮服务和汽车）的总销售额。核心零售销售（不包括汽车/汽油）是衡量消费者支出趋势的更好指标。

- **Explanation**: Reflects consumer health (68%–70% of GDP). Monthly growth >0.5% indicates strong demand (supports consumer stocks); negative growth signals weak confidence. Critical for assessing expansion sustainability.
  - **解释**：反映消费者健康状况（占GDP的68%–70%）。月度增长率>0.5%表明需求强劲（支撑消费股）；负增长则表明信心不足。对于评估扩张的可持续性至关重要。

### 10-Year Treasury Yield & Yield Curve (10Y-2Y Spread) | 10年期国债收益率及收益率曲线（10年期-2年期利差）

#### [Leading Indicator]
- **Definition**: The 10-year Treasury yield is the benchmark for long-term borrowing costs; the 10Y-2Y spread is the difference between 10-year and 2-year yields—used to predict cycle turning points.
- **定义**: 10年期国债收益率是长期借款成本的基准；10年期与2年期国债收益率利差是10年期和2年期国债收益率之间的差值，用于预测周期转折点。
- **Explanation**: An inverted spread (10Y < 2Y) has preceded every U.S. recession since 1950 (6–18-month lag). A steepening spread signals expansion expectation. The 10-year yield benchmarks mortgages/corporate bonds.
- **解释**: 自1950年以来，每次美国经济衰退之前都会出现收益率曲线倒挂（10年期国债收益率低于2年期国债收益率）（滞后6至18个月）。收益率曲线变陡预示着经济扩张预期。10年期国债收益率是抵押贷款和公司债券的基准。

### 1.2 货币政策（Monetary Policy/U.S. Fiscal Policy）

This chapter is designed for firm employees to understand the Federal Reserve's (Fed) interest rate policies, specifically rate hikes and cuts. It also covers the objectives and implementation of the U.S. fiscal policy.

本章旨在帮助企业员工了解美联储（Fed）的利率政策，特别是加息和降息。它还涵盖了美国财政政策的目标和实施情况。

### 1.2.1 美联储货币政策概述（Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Overview）

The Federal Reserve, through the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC), sets the target range for the federal funds rate—the interest rate at which banks lend reserves to each other overnight. This rate influences broader borrowing costs across the economy, including mortgages, corporate loans, and consumer credit. The Fed's dual mandate is to promote maximum employment and stable prices (targeting 2% inflation).

美联储通过联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）设定联邦基金利率的目标区间，即银行之间隔夜拆借准备金的利率。这一利率影响着整个经济中更广泛的借贷成本，包括抵押贷款、企业贷款和消费信贷。美联储的双重使命是促进充分就业和稳定物价（目标通胀率为2%）。

Historical fed funds rate cycles since 1980 include multiple hikes and cuts, often in response to economic conditions.
