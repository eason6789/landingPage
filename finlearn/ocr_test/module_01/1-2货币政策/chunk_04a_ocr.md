1.4.1 美国应对经济周期波动的政策（U.S. Policy Responses to Business Cycle Fluctuations）

Monetary Policy (Federal Reserve) | 货币政策（美联储）
- Expansion/Inflationary Pressures: Fed hikes the federal funds rate to increase borrowing costs, cool economic growth, and curb inflation (e.g., 2022–2023 rate hikes: 0.25% to 5.25% to combat post-COVID inflation). QT (reducing Fed’s bond holdings) is used to tighten liquidity, pressuring long-term bond yields and equity valuations.
- 扩张/通胀压力：美联储上调联邦基金利率以提高借贷成本、抑制经济增长并遏制通胀（例如，2022 - 2023年加息：从0.25%上调至5.25%，以应对新冠疫情后的通胀）。量化紧缩（减少美联储的债券持有量）用于收紧流动性，对长期债券收益率和股票估值造成压力。
- Contraction/Recession: Fed cuts the federal funds rate to lower borrowing costs, stimulate spending and investment (e.g., 2020 rate cut to near 0% during COVID recession). QE (purchasing Treasury and mortgage-backed securities) injects liquidity, lowers long-term yields, and supports asset prices.
- 经济收缩/衰退：美联储下调联邦基金利率以降低借贷成本，刺激消费和投资（例如，2020年新冠衰退期间将利率降至接近0%）。QE（购买国债和抵押支持证券）注入流动性，降低长期收益率，并支撑资产价格。

Fiscal Policy (U.S. Government) | 财政政策（美国政府）
- Contraction/Recession: Expansionary fiscal policy—tax cuts (e.g., 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act) and increased government spending (e.g., 2020 CARES Act, $2.2 trillion in stimulus) to boost aggregate demand. This increases U.S. Treasury issuance, potentially pressuring bond yields if supply outpaces demand.
- 收缩/衰退：扩张性财政政策——减税（例如2017年《减税与就业法案》）和增加政府支出（例如2020年《新冠肺炎救助、救济和经济安全法案》，2.2万亿美元的经济刺激计划）以提振总需求。这会增加美国国债的发行量，如果供应超过需求，可能会给债券收益率带来压力。
- Expansion/High Debt: Restrictive fiscal policy (rarely used in practice) — tax hikes or spending cuts to reduce budget deficits. The U.S. often runs deficits even during expansions, impacting long-term Treasury supply.
- 扩张期/高债务：紧缩性财政政策（实际中很少使用）——增税或削减开支以减少预算赤字。美国即使在扩张期也经常出现赤字，这影响了长期国债的供应。

1.4.2 美国金融市场在完整一个经济周期中的表现（U.S. Financial Market Performance Across the Business Cycle）

Critical for investment decisions—asset class returns vary systematically across cycle stages.
对投资决策至关重要——资产类别回报在周期各阶段存在系统性差异。

膨胀阶段 Expansion Stage
- Stocks: Outperform other assets. CFA Curriculum notes growth stocks (e.g., technology, consumer discretionary) tend to lead as corporate profits rise—S&P 500 average annual return: 14%–16% during expansions.
- 股票：表现优于其他资产。CFA课程指出，成长型股票（如科技、非必需消费品）往往会在企业利润上升时领涨——标准普尔500指数在经济扩张期间的年均回报率为14% - 16%。
- Bonds: Moderate returns. Short-term bonds outperform long-term bonds as the Fed may hike rates to curb inflation.
- 债券：回报适中。由于美联储可能加息以抑制通胀，短期债券表现优于长期债券。
- Commodities: Strong returns. Industrial metals (e.g., copper) benefit from increased business investment; energy prices rise with consumer and industrial demand.
- 大宗商品：回报强劲。工业金属（如铜）受益于商业投资的增加；能源价格随消费者和工业需求上升。

高峰至收缩（衰退）阶段 Peak to Contraction (Recession) Stage
- Stocks: Decline sharply. Defensive stocks (consumer staples, healthcare, utilities) outperform as investors seek stability. S&P 500 average annual return: -18% to -22% during recessions.
- 股票：大幅下跌。防御性股票（必需消费品、医疗保健、公用事业）表现优于大盘，因为投资者寻求稳定性。标准普尔500指数在衰退期间的年均回报率为-18%至-22%。
- Bonds: Safe-haven assets. Long-term U.S. Treasuries outperform as the Fed cuts rates—10-year Treasury average yield falls by 1.4%–1.6 percentage points during recessions; prices rise accordingly.
- 债券：避险资产。随着美联储降息，长期美国国债表现出色——在经济衰退期间，10年期国债平均收益率下降1.4% - 1.6个百分点；价格相应上涨。
- Commodities: Underperform. Demand for industrial commodities collapses; energy prices drop amid reduced consumption.
- 大宗商品：表现不佳。工业大宗商品需求崩溃；能源价格在消费减少的情况下下跌。
