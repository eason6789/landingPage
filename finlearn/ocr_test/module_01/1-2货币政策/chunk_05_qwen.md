# Recession

|  | Sharp economic downturn; rising unemployment rate and falling GDP. Examples: 2001, 2008 cycles | Aggressive rate cuts (e.g., more than 200 bps in a rapid manner) | Negative or mixed: Corporate earnings decline; S&P 500 often recovers a drop | Strong rally: yields plummet sharply (e.g., 10-year Treasury yield drops by over 1%), bonds act as a risk hedge; yield curve steepens sharply. |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 衰退 | 经济急剧衰退：失业率上升，GDP下降。示例：2001年、2008年周期 | 激进的降息（例如，迅速降息超过200个基点） | 负面或好坏参半：企业盈利下降。标准普尔500指数往往下跌 | 强劲反弹：收益率大幅下跌（例如10年期国债收益率跌幅超过1%），债券起到风险对冲作用；收益率曲线趋陡。 |

## 1.5 历史案例研究（Historical Case Studies）

### Specific Case Study: 1994–1995 Hike Cycle: Fed raised rates from 3% to 6%; S&P 500 gained 37.4% during the cycle, but 10-year yield rose 63 bps. Soft landing achieved.

具体案例研究：1994 - 1995年加息周期：美联储将利率从3%上调至6%；标普500指数在该周期内上涨37.4%，但10年期国债收益率上升63个基点。实现了软着陆。

### Specific Case Study: 2022–2023 Hike/2024–2025 Cut: Hikes to 5.5% pressured stocks (-20% in 2022); cuts in 2024-2025 saw S&P 500 rebound, yields stabilize at around 4%.

具体案例研究：2022 - 2023年加息/2024 - 2025年降息：加息至5.5%对股市造成压力（2022年下跌20%）；2024 - 2025年的降息使标准普尔500指数反弹，收益率稳定在4%左右。
