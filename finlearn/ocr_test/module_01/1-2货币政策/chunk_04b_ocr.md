从低谷期到早期扩张阶段 Trough to Early Expansion Stage
- Stocks: Rebound strongly. Cyclical stocks (e.g., industrials, financials) lead as economic optimism returns—S&P 500 average return in the first 12 months post-trough: 23%–27% (CFA textbook data).
- 股票：强劲反弹。随着经济乐观情绪回归，周期性股票（如工业、金融）领涨——触底后前12个月标准普尔500指数平均回报率：23% - 27%（CFA教科书数据）。
- Bonds: Still positive but underperform stocks. Short-term bonds gain as rates stabilize; long-term bonds return moderate.
- 债券：仍为正收益，但表现不及股票。短期债券因利率企稳而上涨；长期债券回报适中。
- Commodities: Rebound. Industrial metals lead as business investment resumes; energy prices rise with economic activity.
- 大宗商品：反弹。随着商业投资的恢复，工业金属领涨；能源价格随经济活动上升。

1.4.3 货币政策对美国股市的影响 Impacts of monetary policy on U.S. Equities (S&P 500)
Equities are sensitive to rate changes due to effects on corporate earnings, discount rates, and investor sentiment.
由于对企业盈利、贴现率和投资者情绪的影响，股票对利率变化较为敏感。
- During Rate Hikes: Stocks often face headwinds as higher borrowing costs compress profit margins and slow growth. The S&P 500 averaged monthly returns of 0.88% from 1991-2015, but performance varied; in rising rate periods, returns were higher (1.26%) than in declining periods (0.73%), though volatility increased. In 2022, amid hikes, the S&P 500 fell nearly 20%. However, early-stage hikes can see continued rallies if fundamentals remain strong. (Source: Mackenzie Investments)
- 加息期间：股票往往面临逆风，因为较高的借贷成本会压缩利润率并减缓增长。1991年至2015年期间，标准普尔500指数的月平均回报率为0.88%，但表现各不相同：在加息周期中，回报率（1.26%）高于降息周期（0.73%），尽管波动性有所增加。2022年，在加息背景下，标准普尔500指数下跌近20%。不过，如果基本面保持强劲，加息初期可能会出现持续反弹。（来源：麦肯齐投资）
- During Rate Cuts: Equities generally perform well in non-recessionary environments, with average 1-year returns of almost 20% post-first cut in soft landings. In recessionary cuts, returns can turn negative. Since 1974, the S&P 500 rallied in most 1-year periods post-first cut, but fell during recessions (e.g., 2001, 2008). In 2024-2025 cuts, the S&P 500 gained amid constructive growth. (Source: Mackenzie Investments)
- 降息期间：在非衰退环境中，股票通常表现良好，在软着陆情况下首次降息后1年的平均回报率接近20%。在衰退性降息中，回报率可能转为负值。自1974年以来，标准普尔500指数在首次降息后的多数1年期内上涨，但在衰退期间下跌（例如2001年、2008年）。在2024 - 2025年的降息中，标准普尔500指数在建设性增长中实现了上涨。（来源：麦肯齐投资）

1.4.4 货币政策对美国国债的影响 Impacts of monetary on U.S. Treasuries (10-Year Yield and Bonds)
Bonds move inversely to yields; rate changes affect prices and the yield curve.
债券价格与收益率呈反向变动；利率变化会影响价格和收益率曲线。
- During Rate Hikes: Bond prices fall as yields rise to compete with new issues. The 10-Year Treasury yield increased by an average of 53 basis points during the last four hike cycles. In 2022, yields surged from around 1.5% to over 4%, causing bond losses.
- 加息期间：债券价格下跌，因为收益率上升以与新发行债券竞争。在过去四个加息周期中，10年期国债收益率平均上升53个基点。2022年，收益率从约1.5%飙升至超过4%，导致债券出现亏损。
- During Rate Cuts: Bonds rally as yields fall, especially longer-duration ones. The yield curve often steepens, with short-end yields dropping more
- 降息期间：债券因收益率下降而上涨，尤其是长期债券。收益率曲线往往会变陡，短期收益率下降幅度更大

1.4.5 情景分析：软着陆vs.经济衰退 (Scenario Analysis: Soft Landing vs. Recession)
Rate effects vary by context—cuts in a soft landing (gradual slowdown without recession) differ from those in a recession.
利率调整的效果因环境而异——软着陆（经济逐渐放缓但不衰退）期间的降息与衰退期间的降息有所不同。
In soft landings, both assets benefit; in recessions, bonds provide diversification as equities weaken.
在软着陆情况下，资产和债券都受益；在衰退期间，随着股票走弱，债券可提供分散投资的作用。
