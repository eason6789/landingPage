# 1.2 货币政策 (Monetary Policy/U.S. Fiscal Policy)

This chapter is designed for firm employees to understand the Federal Reserve's (Fed) interest rate policies, specifically rate hikes and cuts. It also covers the objectives and implementation of the U.S. fiscal policy.

本章旨在帮助企业员工了解美联储（Fed）的利率政策，特别是加息和降息。它还涵盖了美国财政政策的目标和实施情况。

## 1.2.1 美联储货币政策概述 (Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Overview)

The Federal Reserve, through the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC), sets the target range for the federal funds rate—the interest rate at which banks lend reserves to each other overnight. This rate influences broader borrowing costs across the economy, including mortgages, corporate loans, and consumer credit. The Fed's dual mandate is to promote maximum employment and stable prices (targeting 2% inflation).

美联储通过联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）设定联邦基金利率的目标区间，即银行之间隔夜拆借准备金的利率。这一利率影响着整个经济中资产之间的借贷成本，包括抵押贷款、企业贷款和消费信贷。美联储的双重使命是促进充分就业和稳定物价（目标通胀率为2%）。

Historical fed funds rate cycles since 1980 include multiple hikes and cuts, often in response to economic conditions. For instance:

自1980年以来，联邦基金利率的历史周期包括多次加息和降息，通常是基于经济状况的回应。例如：

- **Hike Cycles**: Examples include 1994–1995 (from 3% to 6%), 1999–2000 (4.75% to 6.5%), 2004–2006 (1% to 5.25%), 2015–2018 (0.25% to 2.5%), and 2022–2023 (0.25% to 5.5%).

  - **加息周期**：例如1994 – 1995年（从3%到6%）、1999 – 2000年（4.75%到6.5%）、2004 – 2006年（1%到5.25%）、2015 – 2018年（0.25%到2.5%）以及2022 – 2023年（0.25%到5.5%）。

- **Cut Cycles**: Notable periods include 2001 (6.5% to 1.75%), 2007–2008 (5.25% to 0–0.25%), 2019 (2.5% to 1.75%), and 2024–2025 (5.5% to approximately 4–4.25% by late 2025).

  - **降息周期**：值得注意的时期包括2001年（从6.5%降至1.75%）、2007 – 2008年（从5.25%降至0 – 0.25%）、2019年（从2.5%降至1.75%）以及2024 – 2025年（到2025年底从5.5%降至约4 – 4.25%）。

These cycles reflect the Fed's reactive approach: tightening during expansions to curb inflation and easing during slowdowns to stimulate growth.

这些周期反映了美联储的应对策略：在经济扩张期间收紧政策以抑制通胀，在经济放缓期间放松政策以刺激增长。
