# 1.2 货币政策 (Monetary Policy/U.S. Fiscal Policy)

This chapter is designed for firm employees to understand the Federal Reserve's (Fed) interest rate policies, specifically rate hikes and cuts. It also covers the objectives and implementation of the U.S. fiscal policy.

本章旨在帮助企业员工了解美联储（Fed）的利率政策，特别是加息和降息。它还涵盖了美国财政政策的目标和实施情况。

## 1.2.1 美联储货币政策概述 (Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Overview)

The Federal Reserve, through the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC), sets the target range for the federal funds rate—the interest rate at which banks lend reserves to each other overnight. This rate influences broader borrowing costs across the economy, including mortgages, corporate loans, and consumer credit. The Fed's dual mandate is to promote maximum employment and stable prices (targeting 2% inflation).

美联储通过联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）设定联邦基金利率的目标区间，即银行之间隔夜拆借准备金的利率。这一利率影响着整个经济中资产之间的借贷成本，包括抵押贷款、企业贷款和消费信贷。美联储的双重使命是促进充分就业和稳定物价（目标通胀率为2%）。

Historical fed funds rate cycles since 1980 include multiple hikes and cuts, often in response to economic conditions. For instance:

自1980年以来，联邦基金利率的历史周期包括多次加息和降息，通常是基于经济状况的回应。例如：

- **Hike Cycles**: Examples include 1994–1995 (from 3% to 6%), 1999–2000 (4.75% to 6.5%), 2004–2006 (1% to 5.25%), 2015–2018 (0.25% to 2.5%), and 2022–2023 (0.25% to 5.5%).

  - **加息周期**：例如1994 – 1995年（从3%到6%）、1999 – 2000年（4.75%到6.5%）、2004 – 2006年（1%到5.25%）、2015 – 2018年（0.25%到2.5%）以及2022 – 2023年（0.25%到5.5%）。

- **Cut Cycles**: Notable periods include 2001 (6.5% to 1.75%), 2007–2008 (5.25% to 0–0.25%), 2019 (2.5% to 1.75%), and 2024–2025 (5.5% to approximately 4–4.25% by late 2025).

  - **降息周期**：值得注意的时期包括2001年（从6.5%降至1.75%）、2007 – 2008年（从5.25%降至0 – 0.25%）、2019年（从2.5%降至1.75%）以及2024 – 2025年（到2025年底从5.5%降至约4 – 4.25%）。

These cycles reflect the Fed's reactive approach: tightening during expansions to curb inflation and easing during slowdowns to stimulate growth.

这些周期反映了美联储的应对策略：在经济扩张期间收紧政策以抑制通胀，在经济放缓期间放松政策以刺激增长。

1.2.3 How These Tools Interact in Policy Implementation | 这些工具在政策执行中的相互作用

The Fed uses these tools in concert within its ample-reserves regime:

美联储在其充足准备金制度下协同使用这些工具：

- Setting the target range: The FOMC announces a target range for the federal funds rate (e.g., 5.25–5.50%).
- 设定目标区间：联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）宣布联邦基金利率的目标区间（例如，5.25% - 5.50%）。

- Floor system: IORB and ONRRP keep rates from falling below the target by providing attractive returns on reserves and repo transactions.
- 利率下限体系：IORB和ONRRP通过为准备金和回购交易提供吸引的回报，防止利率跌破目标水平。

- Ceiling and adjustments: The discount rate caps rates from rising too high, while OMO adds or removes reserves as needed for fine-tuning.
- 上限和调整：贴现率上限可防止利率升得过高，而OMO则根据需要增加或减少准备金以进行微调。

- Quantitative tools: In extraordinary times, large-scale OMO (QE or quantitative tightening) expands or shrinks the balance sheet to influence longer-term rates and asset prices.
- 量化工具：在非常时期，大规模的OMO（QE或量化紧缩）会扩大或收缩资产负债表，以影响长期利率和资产价格。

1.3 美国财政政策（Introduction to U.S. Fiscal Policy）

1.3.1 基础概念（Policy Logic and Key Concepts）

Fiscal policy refers to the decisions made by the U.S. government regarding taxation and spending to influence the overall economy. It is managed primarily by Congress and the President, with the goal of achieving macroeconomic stability, sustainable growth, full employment, and price stability (i.e., controlling inflation). Unlike monetary policy, which is handled by the Federal Reserve through interest rates and money supply, fiscal policy uses the federal budget as its main tool. The underlying logic is that government actions can smooth out economic fluctuations, support long-term prosperity, and address inequalities.

财政政策是指美国政府在税收和支出方面所做的决策，旨在影响整体经济。它主要由国会和总统管理，目标是实现宏观经济稳定、可持续增长、充分就业和物价稳定（即控制通货膨胀）。与由美联储通过利率和货币供应量实施的货币政策不同，财政政策以联邦预算作为主要工具。其基本逻辑是，政府行动可以平抑经济波动，支持长期繁荣，并解决不平等问题。

1.3.2 核心目标（Core Objectives of U.S. Fiscal Policy）| 美国财政政策的核心目标（Core Objectives of U.S. Fiscal Policy）

The policy logic is rooted in Keynesian economics, which argues that governments should actively intervene to manage demand in the economy. The main goals include:

政策逻辑源于凯恩斯经济学，该理论主张政府应积极干预以管理经济中的需求。主要目标包括：

- Economic Growth: Stimulating activity during slowdowns.
- 经济增长：在经济放缓期间刺激经济活动。
- Full Employment: Reducing unemployment by creating jobs.
- 充分就业：通过创造就业机会来减少失业。
- Price Stability: Preventing high inflation or deflation.
- 价格稳定：防止高通胀或通缩。
- Income Redistribution: Using taxes and spending to reduce inequality.
- 收入再分配：利用税收和支出减少不平等。

These objectives are pursued through two primary tools:

这些目标是通过两个主要工具来实现的：

- Taxation: Adjusting tax rates (e.g., income, corporate, or sales taxes) to influence how much money people and businesses have to spend.
- 税收：调整税率（如所得税、公司税或销售税），以影响个人和企业的可支配资金。
- Government Spending: Allocating funds for programs like infrastructure, education, defense, or social welfare.
- 政府支出：为基础设、教育、国防或社会福利等项目分配资金。

The U.S. Treasury Department implements these, often through annual budgets proposed by the President and approved by Congress.

美国财政部通常通过总统提议并经国会批准的年度预算来实施这些措施。

1.3.3 财政政策类型（Types of Fiscal Policy）: Expansionary vs. Contractionary

Fiscal policy can be expansionary (to boost the economy) or contractionary (to cool it down). The choice depends on the economic cycle—boom times vs. recessions.

财政政策可以是扩张性的（以刺激经济）或紧缩性的（以给经济降温）。选择取决于经济周期——繁荣时期与衰退时期。

- Expansionary Fiscal Policy: This involves increasing government spending or cutting taxes to inject more money into the economy, raising aggregate demand (total spending by consumers, businesses, and government). It's used when the economy is weak, such as during high unemployment or low growth.
- 扩张性财政政策：这包括增加政府支出或削减税收，以向经济注入更多资金，提高总需求（消费者、企业和政府的总支出）。当经济疲软时，如在高失业率或低增长期间，会采用这种政策。

- Contractionary Fiscal Policy: This means decreasing spending or raising taxes to reduce aggregate demand, which helps control inflation during economic booms.
- 紧缩性财政政策：这意味着减少支出或提高税收以降低总需求，这有助于在经济繁荣时期控制通货膨胀。

Example: Imagine the economy as a car engine. Expansionary policy is like pressing the gas pedal to speed up (e.g., more spending on roads creates jobs). Contractionary policy is like easing off the gas to avoid overheating (e.g., higher taxes reduce excessive consumer spending).

示例：把经济想象成汽车发动机。扩张性政策就像踩下油门加速（例如，增加道路建设支出可以创造就业机会）。紧缩性政策就像松开油门以避免过热（例如，提高税收可以减少过度的消费者支出）。

1.3.4 逆周期调节（Countercyclical Regulation）: The Key Stabilizing Logic

- A central principle in the U.S. fiscal policy is countercyclical regulation (also called countercyclical fiscal policy). This means the government acts in the opposite direction of the economic cycle to stabilize it:
- 美国财政政策的一项核心原则是反周期调节（也称为反周期财政政策），这意味着政府采取与经济周期相反的行动来稳定经济：

- During a recession (economic downturn), the government adopts expansionary measures to counteract falling demand.
- 在衰退（经济低迷）期间，政府采取扩张性措施来应对需求下降。

- During an expansion (economic boom), it shifts to contractionary measures to prevent overheating, like bubbles in asset prices or runaway inflation.
- 在扩张（经济繁荣）期间，它会转向紧缩措施以防止经济过热，如资产价格泡沫或失控的通货膨胀。

This logic helps avoid extreme swings, promoting steady growth. Automatic stabilizers (built-in features like unemployment benefits that rise during recessions) enhance this, but discretionary actions (deliberate policy changes) are often needed.

<!-- chunk 3 (qwen) -->
这种逻辑有利于避免极端波动，促进稳定增长。自动稳定器（如在经济衰退期间增加的失业救济等内置功能）增强了这一点，但往往还需要相机抉择行动（刺激的政策调整）。

### ✅ Specific Case Study: The 2008–2009 Global Financial Crisis  
**具体案例研究：2008 - 2009年全球金融危机**

**Context:** The U.S. economy entered a severe recession due to the housing market collapse and banking failures. Unemployment soared to 10%, and GDP (gross domestic product) shrank by about 4%.  
**背景：** 美国经济因房地产市场崩溃和银行倒闭而陷入严重衰退。失业率飙升至10%，GDP（国内生产总值）萎缩约4%。

**Countercyclical Response:** Under President Obama, Congress passed the American Recovery and Reinvestment Act (ARRA) in 2009, an expansionary package worth $831 billion. This included:  
**反周期响应：** 在奥巴马总统执政期间，国会于2009年通过了《美国复苏与再投资法案》（ARRA），这是一项价值831亿美元的扩张性计划。其中包括：

- Tax cuts for individuals and businesses to increase disposable income (e.g., payroll tax reductions, putting more money in workers’ pockets).  
  为个人和企业减税以增加可支配收入（例如，削减工资税，让工人兜里有更多钱）。

- Increased spending on infrastructure (e.g., roads, bridges, and schools), creating jobs in construction.  
  增加对基础设施（如道路、桥梁和学校）的支出，从而创造建筑领域的就业机会。

- Extended unemployment benefits, acting as an automatic stabilizer to support consumer spending.  
  延长失业救济金，作为自动稳定器来支持消费者支出。

**Outcome:** This helped shorten the recession, adding millions of jobs and boosting GDP growth.  
**成果：** 这有助于缩短衰退期，增加数百万个就业岗位，并推动GDP增长。经济学家估计，如果没有这些措施，失业率可能会达到12%至15%。这体现了反周期逻辑：当私营部门需求崩溃时，政府介入填补缺口。

### Another Example: The COVID-19 Pandemic Response (2020–2021)  
**另一个例子：新冠疫情应对（2020 – 2021年）**

**Context:** The pandemic caused a sharp economic shutdown, with unemployment jumping to 14.8% in April 2020 and businesses closing en masse.  
**背景：** 疫情导致经济急剧停摆，2020年4月失业率飙升至14.8%，企业大规模倒闭。

**Countercyclical Measures:** Multiple stimulus bills were enacted, totaling over $5 trillion:  
**反周期措施：** 多项刺激法案得以颁布，总额超过5万亿美元：

- The CARES Act (2020) under President Trump provided direct payments ($1,200 per adult) to boost consumer spending, expanded unemployment insurance (adding $600 weekly), and loans to small businesses via the Paycheck Protection Program (PPP).  
  特朗普总统执政期间的《新冠病毒援助、救济和经济安全法案》（2020年）提供了直接支付（每位成年人1200美元）以刺激消费支出，扩大了失业保险（每周增加600美元），并通过薪资保护计划（PPP）向小企业提供贷款。

- The American Rescue Plan (2021) under President Biden added more direct payments ($1,400), child tax credits, and funding for vaccines and schools.  
  拜登总统任内的《美国救援计划》（2021年）增加了更多直接支付（1400美元）、儿童税收抵免，以及疫苗和学校的资金投入。

**Logic in Action:** These expansionary steps countered the cycle by maintaining demand when private activity halted. For instance, direct payments helped families pay bills, preventing deeper poverty and supporting retail sales. Result: The economy recovered faster than expected, with unemployment dropping to 3.9% by late 2021.  
**行动中的逻辑：** 这些扩张性措施在私人活动停滞时通过维持需求来应对经济周期。例如，直接支付帮助家庭支付账单，防止了贫困加剧，并支撑了零售销售。结果：经济复苏速度快于预期，到2021年末失业率降至3.9%。

### 1.3.5 Challenges and Considerations in Fiscal Policy Logic | 财政政策逻辑中的挑战与考量

While effective, fiscal policy isn't perfect:  
虽然财政政策有效，但并非完美无缺：

- **Timing and Political Delays:** Policies take time to implement; Congress debates can slow responses.  
  **时机与政治拖延：** 政策实施需要时间；国会辩论可能会延缓应对措施。

- **Debt Implications:** Expansionary policies often increase the national debt. The logic is that short-term borrowing is justified if it prevents long-term damage, but sustained deficits can lead to higher interest rates.  
  **债务影响：** 扩张性政策往往会增加负债。其逻辑是，如果短期借款能够避免长期损害，那么这种借款就是合理的，但持续的赤字可能导致利率上升。

- **Coordination with Monetary Policy:** Fiscal actions work best alongside Fed policies, like low interest rates during recessions.  
  **与货币政策的协调：** 财政行动与美联储政策（如衰退期间的低利率政策）配合时效果最佳。
