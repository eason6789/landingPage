1.1.1 经济周期基础知识（Fundamentals of the Business Cycle）
Stages of the Business Cycle
The business cycle refers to the recurring, irregular fluctuations in aggregate economic activity around the economy’s long-term potential growth trend. It is officially determined by the U.S. National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER), which defines a "recession" as a significant decline in economic activity spread across the economy, lasting more than a few months—encompassing production, employment, and income. The business cycle’s duration varies (typically 2-10 years) and is driven by shifts in aggregate demand and aggregate supply.
商业周期是指总体经济活动围绕经济长期潜在增长趋势反复出现的、不规则的波动。它由美国国家经济研究局（NBER）正式确定，该局将“衰退”定义为广泛蔓延于整个经济的经济活动显著下降，持续数月以上——包括生产、就业和收入。商业周期的持续时间各不相同（通常为2至10年），由总需求和总供给的变化驱动。

1.1.3 经济周期的关键驱动因素（Key Drivers of the U.S. Business Cycle）
Factors directly influencing U.S. business cycle fluctuations
直接影响美国商业周期波动的因素
- Consumer Spending: Consumer spending accounts for approximately 68%-70% of U.S. GDP, making it the primary driver of aggregate demand. Changes in consumer confidence (tied to employment security and wage growth, per textbook) directly impact retail sales, housing demand, and discretionary spending (e.g., autos, electronics)—critical for U.S. equity sectors like consumer staples and discretionary.
- 消费者支出：消费者支出约占美国GDP的68% - 70%，是总需求的主要驱动力。消费者信心的变化（根据教科书，与就业保障和工资增长相关）直接影响零售销售、住房需求和可自由支配支出（例如，汽车、电子产品）——对美国消费必需品和可自由支配等股票部门至关重要。
