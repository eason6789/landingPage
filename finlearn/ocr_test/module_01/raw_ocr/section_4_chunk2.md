- **Explanation**: High CCI (>100, e.g., 102.6 in 2023) signals strong spending intent (supports retail stocks); low CCI (<80, e.g., current reading of 54) indicates caution. The expectations sub-index is a leading indicator.
- **解释**: 高CCI（>100，例如2023年的102.6）表明强劲的消费意愿（支撑零售股）；低CCI（<80，例如当前读数为54）表明需谨慎。预期子指数是一个领先指标。

### 10-Year Treasury Yield & Yield Curve (10Y-2Y Spread) | 10年期国债收益率及收益率曲线（10年期-2年期利差）

#### [Leading Indicator]
- **Definition**: The 10-year Treasury yield is the benchmark for long-term borrowing costs; the 10Y-2Y spread is the difference between 10-year and 2-year yields—used to predict cycle turning points.
- **定义**: 10年期国债收益率是长期借款成本的基准；10年期与2年期国债收益率利差是10年期和2年期国债收益率之间的差值，用于预测周期转折点。
- **Explanation**: An inverted spread (10Y < 2Y) has preceded every U.S. recession since 1950 (6–18-month lag). A steepening spread signals expansion expectation. The 10-year yield benchmarks mortgages/corporate bonds.
- **解释**: 自1950年以来，每次美国经济衰退之前都会出现收益率曲线倒挂（10年期国债收益率低于2年期国债收益率）（滞后6至18个月）。收益率曲线变陡预示着经济扩张预期。10年期国债收益率是抵押贷款和公司债券的基准。

### 1.2.1 美联储货币政策概述（Federal Reserve Monetary Policy Overview）

The Federal Reserve, through the Federal Open Market Committee (FOMC), sets the target range for the federal funds rate—the interest rate at which banks lend reserves to each other overnight. This rate influences broader borrowing costs across the economy, including mortgages, corporate loans, and consumer credit. The Fed's dual mandate is to promote maximum employment and stable prices (targeting 2% inflation).

美联储通过联邦公开市场委员会（FOMC）设定联邦基金利率的目标区间，即银行之间隔夜拆借准备金的利率。这一利率影响着整个经济中更广泛的借贷成本，包括抵押贷款、企业贷款和消费信贷。美联储的双重使命是促进充分就业和稳定物价（目标通胀率为2%）。

Historical fed funds rate cycles since 1980 include multiple hikes and cuts, often in response to economic conditions.
