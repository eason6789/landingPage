
（与就业保障和工资增长相关）直接影响零售销售、住房需求和可自由支配支出（如汽车、电子）品），这是美国的必需消费品和非必需消费品等股票板块至关重要。

Business Investment: Includes spending on machinery, equipment, and commercial real estate. U.S. business investment is sensitive to interest rates (Fed policy), corporate profit margins, and technological innovation (e.g., AI-related investment boom in 2023–2024).

企业投资：包括对机械、设备和商业地产的支出。美国商业投资对利率（美联储政策）、企业利润率和技术创新（例如，2023 - 2024年与AI相关的投资热潮）敏感。

Monetary Policy (Federal Reserve): Fed’s adjustments to the federal funds rate and balance sheet (quantitative easing/QE, quantitative tightening/QT) directly influence borrowing costs for businesses and consumers, asset prices (stocks, bonds), and inflation.

货币政策（美联储）：美联储对联邦基金利率和资产负债表（量化宽松/QE、量化紧缩/QT）的调整直接影响企业和消费者的借贷成本、资产价格（股票、债券）以及通货膨胀。

Net Exports: Tied to the U.S. dollar exchange rate and global demand. A stronger USD (e.g., 2022) reduces U.S. export competitiveness, while weak global demand (e.g., 2023 Eurozone slowdown) weighs on U.S. export growth.

净出口：与美元汇率和全球需求相关。美元走强（如2022年）会削弱美国出口竞争力，而全球需求疲软（如2023年欧元区经济放缓）则会拖累美国出口增长。

美国金融市场密切关注这些指标，以评估商业周期阶段、通货膨胀和政策趋势。每个指标都包含与市场相关的解释：

Gross Domestic Product (GDP) | 国内生产总值 (GDP)
[Coincident Indicator]
[同步指标]
- Definition: The total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country’s borders in a given period. The BEA releases quarterly data with three revisions; real GDP (adjusted for inflation) is the key metric for cycle analysis (nominal GDP is less useful for trend identification).

定义：在给定时期内，一个国家境内生产的所有最终商品和服务的总市场价值。美国经济分析局（BEA）每季度发布数据，并有三次修订；实际GDP（经通胀调整）是周期分析的关键指标（名义GDP在趋势识别方面的作用较小）。

- Explanation: The broadest measure of economic activity. Real GDP growth above 2% (U.S. potential growth rate) indicates expansion; growth below 1% signals slowing activity; negative growth for ≥2 quarters is a common recession proxy (supplementary to NBER’s definition). Market reactions: strong GDP may raise Fed hike expectations (pressuring bonds); weak growth may signal easing (bonds outperform).

解释：经济活动最广泛的衡量指标。实际GDP增长高于2%（美国潜在增长率）表明经济扩张；增长低于1%则预示经济活动放缓；连续两个或更多季度的负增长是常见的衰退指标（作为美国国家经济研究局定义的补充）。市场反应：强劲的GDP可能提高美联储加息预期（给债券带来压力）；经济疲软可能预示政策宽松（债券表现优于其他资产）。

Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP) | 非农就业人数 (NFP)
[Coincident Indicator]
[同步指标]
- Definition: Monthly BLS data measuring the change in employment across all sectors except agriculture, private households, and non-profits—considered the most reliable gauge of U.S. labor market trends.

定义：每月BLS数据，衡量除农业、私人家庭和非营利组织之外所有行业的就业变化，被认为是衡量美国劳动力市场趋势最可靠的指标。

- Explanation: NFP is the most impactful monthly indicator. Average monthly gains of 150k–200k signal stable employment; gains >300k indicate tight labor markets; gains <100k suggest slowing growth. The changes in NFP directly links to consumer spending (68%–70% of GDP) and Fed policy.

解释：非农就业人数（NFP）是最具影响力的月度指标。月均新增15万至20万个就业岗位表明就业形势稳定；新增超过30万个就业岗位则表明劳动力市场紧张；涨幅<10万，表明放缓增长。非农就业人数的变化直接关系到消费者支出（占GDP的68% - 70%）和美联储政策。

Unemployment Rate | 失业率
[Lagging Indicator]
[滞后指标]
- Definition: BLS monthly data measuring the percentage of the labor force (employed + actively seeking work) that is unemployed; excludes discouraged workers (a key limitation noted in the textbook).

定义：BLS月度数据，用于衡量劳动力（就业者+积极求职人员）中失业人员的百分比；不包括灰心丧气的劳动者（这是教科书中提到的一个关键局限性）。

- Explanation: The U.S. natural rate of unemployment (full employment) is 4%–4.5%. Unemployment below this range creates wage inflation pressures; above 6% (e.g., 14.7% in 2020) signals recessionary conditions.

解释：美国自然失业率（充分就业）为4% - 4.5%，低于这一范围的失业率会产生工资通胀压力；高于6%（例如2020年为14.7%）表明经济处于衰退状态。
