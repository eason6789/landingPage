These indicators are closely monitored by U.S. financial markets to gauge business cycle stages, inflation, and policy trends. Each includes a market-relevant explanation:

美国金融市场密切关注这些指标，以评估商业周期阶段、通胀膨胀和政策趋势。每个指标都包含与市场相关的解释：

---

**Gross Domestic Product (GDP) | 国内生产总值 (GDP)**  
**[Coincident Indicator]**  
**[同步指标]**

- **Definition**: The total market value of all final goods and services produced within a country’s borders in a given period. The BEA releases quarterly data with three revisions: real GDP (adjusted for inflation) is the key metric for cycle analysis (nominal GDP is less useful for trend identification).
- **定义**：在给定时期内，一个国家境内生产的所有最终商品和服务的总市场价值。美国经济分析局（BEA）每季度发布数据，并有三次修订；实际GDP（经通胀调整）是周期分析的关键指标（名义GDP在趋势识别方面的作用较小）。
- **Explanation**: The broadest measure of economic activity. Real GDP growth above 2% (U.S. potential growth rate) indicates expansion; growth below 1% signals slowing activity; negative growth for ≥2 quarters is a common recession proxy (supplementary to NBER’s definition). Market reactions: strong GDP may raise Fed hike expectations (pressuring bonds); weak growth may signal easing (bonds outperform).
- **解释**：经济活动最广泛的衡量指标。实际GDP增长率高于2%（美国潜在增长率）表明经济扩张；增长率低于1%则预示经济活动放缓；连续两个或更多季度的负增长是常见的衰退指标（作为美国国家经济研究局定义的补充）。市场反应：强劲的GDP可能会提高美联储加息预期（给债券带来压力）；经济增长乏力可能预示着政策宽松（债券表现优于其他资产）。

---

**Nonfarm Payrolls (NFP) | 非农就业人数（NFP）**  
**[Coincident Indicator]**  
**[同步指标]**

- **Definition**: Monthly BLS data measuring the change in employment across all sectors except agriculture, private households, and non-profits—considered the most reliable gauge of U.S. labor market trends.
- **定义**：每月BLS数据，衡量除农业、私人家庭和非营利组织之外所有行业的就业变化，被认为是衡量美国劳动力市场趋势最可靠的指标。
- **Explanation**: NFP is the most impactful monthly signal. Average monthly gains of 150k–200k signal stable employment; gains >300k indicate tight labor markets; gains <100k suggest slowing growth. The changes in NFP directly links to consumer spending (68%–70% of GDP) and Fed policy.
- **解释**：非农就业人数（NFP）是最具影响力的月度指标。月均新增15万至20万个就业岗位表明就业形势稳定；新增超过30万个就业岗位则表明劳动力市场紧张；涨幅<10万，表明放缓增长。非农就业人数的变化直接关系到消费者支出（占GDP的68%–70%）和美联储政策。

---

**Unemployment Rate | 失业率**  
**[Lagging Indicator]**  
**[滞后指标]**

- **Definition**: BLS monthly data measuring the percentage of the labor force (employed + unemployed).
- **定义**：BLS月度数据，用于衡量劳动力（就业者+积极求职人员）中失业人员的百分比；不包括灰心丧气的劳动者（这是教科书中提到的一个关键局限性）。
- **Explanation**: The U.S. natural rate of unemployment (full employment) is 4%–4.5%. Unemployment below this range creates wage inflation pressures; above 6% (e.g., 14.7% in 2020) signals recessionary conditions.
- **解释**：美国自然失业率（充分就业）为4%–4.5%，低于这一范围的失业率会产生工资通胀压力；高于6%（例如2020年为14.7%）表明经济处于衰退状态。
