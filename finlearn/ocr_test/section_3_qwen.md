# Consumer Price Index (CPI) | 消费者价格指数 (CPI)
## [Lagging Indicator]
### [滞后指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BLS data measuring the change in prices of a fixed basket of goods/services (food, energy, housing, healthcare) purchased by urban consumers. Core CPI (excludes food/energy) is preferred for trend analysis due to volatility (textbook clarification).
  - **定义**：每月BLS数据，用于衡量城市消费者购买的一篮子固定商品/服务（食品、能源、住房、医疗保健）价格的变化。由于波动性，核心CPI（不包括食品/能源）更适合用于趋势分析（教科书说明）。

- **Explanation**: The primary market-watched inflation gauge. Core CPI growth above 3% triggers Fed hawkishness, below 1% raises deflation risks. Critical for bonds (inflation erodes fixed returns) and stocks (high inflation compresses margins).
  - **解释**：主要受市场关注的通胀指标。核心CPI涨幅超过3%会引发美联储的鹰派立场；低于1%会增加通缩风险。这对债券（通胀会侵蚀固定回报）和股票（高通胀会压缩利润率）至关重要。

# Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) Price Index | 个人消费支出 (PCE) 价格指数
## [Lagging Indicator]
### [滞后指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BEA data measuring prices of goods/services purchased by consumers, with a more flexible basket than CPI (e.g., includes employer-paid healthcare). Core PCE (excludes food/energy) is the Fed’s preferred inflation gauge.
  - **定义**：美国经济分析局（BEA）的月度数据，用于衡量消费者购买商品和服务的价格，其商品篮子比CPI更灵活（例如，包括雇主支付的医疗保险费用）。核心个人消费支出（PCE）（不包括食品/能源）是美联储首选的通胀指标。

- **Explanation**: Directly guides Fed policy (2% target). Core PCE above 2% sustains hawkishness; below 2% supports dovishness. PCE is less volatile than CPI, leading to more muted but longer-lasting market reactions.
  - **解释**：直接指导美联储政策（2%目标）。核心PCE高于2%会维持鹰派立场；低于2%的支持鸽派立场。PCE的波动性低于CPI，导致市场反应更为温和但持续时间更长。

# ISM Manufacturing PMI | ISM制造业PMI
## [Leading Indicator]
### [领先指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly ISM survey of manufacturing firms, measuring new orders, production, employment, supplier deliveries, and inventories. A reading >50 indicates expansion; <50 indicates contraction.
  - **定义**：每月对制造业企业进行的ISM调查，衡量新订单、生产、就业、供应商交货和库存情况。读数>50表示扩张；<50表示收缩。

- **Explanation**: A leading indicator (PMI >55 signals strong industrial growth; <45 suggests manufacturing recession). The new orders sub-index is a key predictor of future GDP growth.
  - **解释**：领先指标（PMI>55表明工业强劲增长；<45则暗示制造业衰退）。新订单分项指数是未来GDP增长的关键预测指标。

# Retail Sales | 零售销售
## [Coincident Indicator]
### [同步指标]

- **Definition**: Monthly BLS data measuring total sales of retail establishments (including food services and autos). Core retail sales (excludes autos/gasoline) is a better proxy for consumer spending trends.
  - **定义**：每月BLS数据，衡量零售企业（包括餐饮服务和汽车）的总销售额。核心零售销售（不包括汽车/汽油）是衡量消费者支出趋势的更好指标。

- **Explanation**: Reflects consumer health (68%–70% of GDP). Monthly growth >0.5% indicates strong demand (supports consumer stocks); negative growth signals weak confidence. Critical for assessing expansion sustainability.
  - **解释**：反映消费者健康状况（占GDP的68%–70%）。月度增长率>0.5%表明需求强劲（支撑消费股）；负增长则表明信心不足。对于评估扩张的可持续性至关重要。
